Boardly — MCQ Practice for FSC Matric & Post FSC Students

Self-Paced Diagnostic MCQ Practice & Test Prep

Boardly is an online MCQ practice and test preparation platform built for Pakistani students. It is designed to foster active recall, conceptual clarity, and disciplined exam habits. Learners access an extensive repository of chapter-wise questions, timed mock tests, and step-by-step solutions across science and general disciplines.

Use the platform to revise textbook chapters, solve past papers, and improve problem-solving speed under realistic conditions. Detailed diagnostic telemetry helps you spot learning gaps early and master your syllabus with confidence.

Academic Foundation: SHS Virtual Academy & schaorlio.me

Boardly is an educational initiative developed and operated under SHS Virtual Academy (shsvirtualacademy.pro). Our parent academy is dedicated to empowering Pakistani learners with modern digital study tools. It provides overarching curriculum guidance, academic direction, and student mentorship.

Meanwhile, Boardly serves as the specialized self-assessment and testing engine. Please note that live scheduled lectures, interactive video classes, and instructor-led mentoring sessions are hosted separately through schaorlio.me. This allows Boardly to focus exclusively on high-yield question drills, answer derivations, and diagnostic testing.

Specialized Academic & Competitive Exam Tracks

Our practice modules and test banks are tailored to the exact requirements of Pakistani curricula. Each track addresses the specific cognitive demands of secondary, intermediate, and university entrance examinations:

Matric Students (Classes 9 & 10 / FBISE & Provincial SSC)

Access comprehensive chapter-wise question banks for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Computer Science, English, Urdu, and Islamiat. Every question is mapped directly to Federal Board (FBISE) Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs) and provincial standards. This targeted alignment helps 9th and 10th class students build foundational reasoning and problem-solving skills early.

Intermediate Students (Classes 11 & 12 / FSC, ICS & HSSC)

Explore rigorous practice sets for Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, ICS, and General Science streams. The bank features high-level numerical derivations, organic reaction mechanisms, calculus integrals, and computer programming logic. All questions match standard intermediate board examination standards.

Medical College Entrance Aspirants (PMDC MDCAT)

Prepare for medical college admission with tests aligned strictly with PMDC syllabus weightings. Practice high-yield questions across Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English, and Logical Reasoning. These timed sets train pattern recognition, option elimination, and high-speed problem solving under exam pressure.

Engineering University Entrance Aspirants (UET Taxila TCAT)

Practice for engineering entry tests using our simulated 100-question, 400-mark examination format. The engine includes realistic negative marking penalties to test your risk management. Content focuses heavily on advanced calculus, Newtonian mechanics, electromagnetism, and physical chemistry calculations.

Core Testing Features & AI-Powered Practice Tools

Boardly replaces passive textbook reading with active testing, instant feedback, and diagnostic analytics:

SLO-Aligned Chapter-Wise Question Banks

Test every textbook chapter systematically after your daily lectures. Questions are classified across three cognitive levels: knowledge, conceptual understanding, and numerical application. This classification guarantees thorough coverage of the complete syllabus.

Timed Mock Exam Simulations & Negative Marking

Replicate real exam conditions with full-length timed tests. Practice pacing yourself, develop mental stamina, and overcome test anxiety. Built-in countdown timers and realistic layouts prepare you thoroughly for test day.

Instant Scoring Telemetry & Pacing Analytics

Get instant grading with detailed accuracy metrics and pacing indicators. See your average time spent per question to optimize time allocation. For entrance tests like TCAT, scoring automatically accounts for negative marking deductions.

Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivations & Solutions

Every question provides a clear, step-by-step solution and formula breakdown. Scientific justifications explain why the correct option applies and why distractors are wrong. This approach ensures you understand core concepts rather than memorizing answers.

24/7 AI Study Buddy Tutor for Concept Explanations

Get on-demand assistance whenever you encounter challenging numericals, definitions, or mechanisms. The integrated AI Study Buddy delivers instant conversational explanations, helpful memory mnemonics, and alternative solution strategies 24/7.

How to Use Boardly Effectively for Maximum Exam Retention

Follow this proven 4-step study cycle to turn passive reading into top exam scores:

Step 1: Study Core Textbook Concepts First

Review your textbook chapter thoroughly or attend your lectures first. Solid conceptual foundations from school or SHS Virtual Academy make question practice far more effective.

Step 2: Take Untimed Chapter-Wise Practice Drills

Solve 20 to 30 questions in untimed study mode with immediate feedback enabled. This reveals misunderstandings early and reinforces textbook concepts right after reading.

Step 3: Simulate Full Timed Mock Examinations

Attempt full-length timed mock tests under realistic testing conditions. This trains your speed, builds stress tolerance, and sharpens your decision-making for exam day.

Step 4: Review Mistakes with Step-by-Step AI Explanations

Always review every missed or skipped question carefully. Read the detailed step-by-step derivation or ask Study Buddy AI to clarify difficult steps before moving to the next chapter.
