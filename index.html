Boardly is a comprehensive online MCQ practice and examination preparation platform engineered specifically for students across Pakistan. Built to foster active recall, conceptual clarity, and disciplined test-taking habits, Boardly provides an extensive repository of chapter-wise multiple-choice questions, timed mock tests, and instant step-by-step solutions across science and general disciplines. Whether you are revising core textbook concepts, practicing past papers, or testing your problem-solving speed under realistic exam conditions, Boardly helps you identify learning gaps and master your syllabus with confidence.

Specialized Academic & Competitive Exam Tracks

Our practice modules and test banks are tailored specifically to the unique requirements and cognitive demands of Pakistani secondary, intermediate, and university entrance test curricula:

Matric Students (Classes 9 & 10 / FBISE & Provincial SSC)

Comprehensive topic-by-topic question banks covering Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Computer Science, English, Urdu, and Islamiat. All questions are mapped directly to Federal Board (FBISE) Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs) and provincial board standards, helping younger students build strong foundational reasoning and conceptual problem-solving early.

Intermediate Students (Classes 11 & 12 / FSC, ICS & HSSC)

Rigorous practice sets covering Pre-Medical (Biology, Chemistry, Physics), Pre-Engineering (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry), ICS (Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics), and General Science streams. Features high-level numerical derivations, chemical reaction mechanisms, calculus integrals, and algorithmic logic questions aligned with intermediate board standards.

Medical College Entrance Aspirants (PMDC MDCAT)

Specialized medical test prep track aligned strictly with official PMDC syllabus weightings. Features high-yield multiple-choice questions in Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English, and Logical Reasoning, engineered to build speed, pattern recognition, and elimination strategies under intense time pressure.

Engineering University Entrance Aspirants (UET Taxila TCAT)

Dedicated engineering entry test series structured around the 100-question, 400-mark format with negative marking simulation. Focuses heavily on advanced calculus, Newtonian mechanics, electricity and magnetism, and physical chemistry calculations.
