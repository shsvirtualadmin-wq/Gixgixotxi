Boardly — MCQ Practice for FSC Matric & Post FSC Students

Boardly is a comprehensive online MCQ practice and examination preparation platform engineered specifically for students across Pakistan. Designed to foster active recall and conceptual clarity, Boardly provides an extensive repository of chapter-wise multiple-choice questions, timed mock tests, and instant step-by-step solutions across science and general subjects. Whether you are revising core textbook concepts, practicing past papers, or testing your problem-solving speed under strict exam conditions, Boardly helps you identify learning gaps and master your syllabus with confidence.

Boardly is an educational initiative developed and operated under SHS Virtual Academy (shsvirtualacademy.pro), our parent academic organization dedicated to empowering Pakistani learners with modern digital learning tools. While our main academy coordinates curriculum guidance, academic direction, and student mentorship, Boardly serves as the dedicated digital testing and self-assessment engine. Note that live scheduled lectures, interactive video classes, and instructor-led mentoring sessions are conducted separately through schaorlio.me, allowing Boardly to focus exclusively on question practice, instant answer derivations, and diagnostic testing.

Who is Boardly for?

Our practice modules and test banks are tailored specifically to the unique requirements of Pakistani secondary, intermediate, and entrance test curricula:

Matric Students (Classes 9 & 10 / SSC)

Comprehensive topic-by-topic question banks covering Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Computer Science, English, Urdu, and Islamiat. All questions are mapped directly to Federal Board (FBISE) Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs) and provincial board standards to build strong foundational knowledge.

Intermediate Students (Classes 11 & 12 / HSSC / FSC)

Rigorous practice sets covering Pre-Medical (Biology, Chemistry, Physics), Pre-Engineering (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry), ICS (Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics), and General Science streams. Features high-level numerical problems, chemical reaction mechanisms, and conceptual derivations.

Medical Entrance Aspirants (PMDC MDCAT)

Specialized medical test prep track aligned with the official PMDC syllabus. Features high-yield multiple-choice questions in Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and English logical reasoning, designed to build speed and accuracy under intense time pressure.

Engineering Entrance Aspirants (UET Taxila TCAT)

Dedicated engineering entry test series structured around the 100-question, 400-mark format with negative marking simulation. Focuses heavily on advanced calculus, mechanics, electricity and magnetism, and physical chemistry.

How to use Boardly effectively for maximum retention

Follow this proven 4-step study cycle to turn passive reading into top exam scores:

1. Study Core Concepts First

Review your textbook chapter or attend your classes through school or SHS Virtual Academy before attempting practice questions.

2. Untimed Chapter Drills

Solve 20–30 questions in study mode with immediate feedback turned on to uncover conceptual misunderstandings early.

3. Full Timed Mock Exams

Take full timed mock tests under strict exam conditions to train your pacing, stress tolerance, and quick decision-making.

4. Review Mistakes with AI Explanations

Never skip reviewing missed questions. Read the step-by-step breakdown or consult Study Buddy AI to resolve doubts before moving on.
