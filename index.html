Boardly — MCQ Practice for FSC Matric & Post FSC Students

Boardly is a comprehensive online MCQ practice and examination preparation platform engineered specifically for students across Pakistan. Designed to foster active recall and conceptual clarity, Boardly provides an extensive repository of chapter-wise multiple-choice questions, timed mock tests, and instant step-by-step solutions across science and general subjects. Whether you are revising core textbook concepts or testing your problem-solving speed under exam conditions, Boardly helps you identify learning gaps and master your syllabus with confidence.

Boardly is an educational initiative developed under SHS Virtual Academy (shsvirtualacademy.pro), our parent academic organization dedicated to empowering Pakistani learners with modern digital learning tools. While our main academy coordinates curriculum guidance and student mentorship, Boardly serves as the dedicated testing and self-assessment engine. Note that live scheduled lectures and interactive video classes are conducted separately through schaorlio.me, allowing Boardly to focus exclusively on question practice, instant answer derivations, and diagnostic testing.

Who Boardly Is For

Our practice modules are structured specifically for secondary, higher secondary, and post-FSC entry test students:

  • Matric Students (Classes 9 & 10): Thorough topic-by-topic question banks covering Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Computer Science, English, Urdu, and Islamiat aligned with FBISE and provincial board curricula.
  • FSC Intermediate Students (Classes 11 & 12 / HSSC): Specialized practice sets for Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, ICS, and General Science streams with rigorous numerical problems and theoretical questions.
  • Post-FSC & Entrance Aspirants: High-yield question simulations for competitive entrance exams, including PMDC MDCAT for medical admissions and UET Taxila TCAT for engineering university entry.

Key Features & Practice Tools

Boardly is built with tools designed to make self-study efficient, measurable, and engaging:

  • Subject-Wise & Chapter-Wise MCQs: Practice questions organized by chapter and Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs) to systematically cover each topic in your syllabus.
  • Timed Mock Tests & Custom Quizzes: Simulate real board and entry test conditions with customizable question counts, timer countdowns, and automated score calculations.
  • Instant Detailed Explanations: Receive immediate answer keys along with step-by-step mathematical derivations and conceptual explanations for every question.
  • AI Study Buddy: Clarify difficult concepts and ask follow-up questions with our integrated AI learning assistant available around the clock.
  • Performance Analytics: Monitor your accuracy, review incorrect answers, and track subject-wise progress over time to focus your study sessions where they matter most.
